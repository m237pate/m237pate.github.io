Project Overview

The objective of this project was to radically rethink energy harvesting in high-airflow, constrained environments. By replacing a traditional radiator fan assembly with a high-speed, vertical-axis wind turbine, we aimed to capture parasitic energy losses in industrial cooling systems.

The challenge involved complex fluid dynamics modeling to ensure the turbine did not impede necessary cooling airflow while still generating meaningful electrical output. The dual-purpose design required precision engineering of blade profiles to operate efficiently within turbulent wake regions.
